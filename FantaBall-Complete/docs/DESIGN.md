# FantaBall visual identity
Apple-inspired independent design, no Apple affiliation. System typography; large titles; porcelain surfaces and forest green; restrained amber demo badge; automatic light/dark appearance; rounded cards; generous spacing; tabular numeric metrics; persistent labelled bottom tabs; decision-first auction panel; purchase confirmation; readable chat bubbles; 48px controls and visible keyboard focus. Reduced-motion preferences respected. Do not bundle proprietary Apple fonts or icons without permission. No claim of native Liquid Glass. Platform VoiceOver, large-text, contrast, keyboard, safe-area and device testing remains required. Browser demo wrapped in Expo WebView, not SwiftUI.
