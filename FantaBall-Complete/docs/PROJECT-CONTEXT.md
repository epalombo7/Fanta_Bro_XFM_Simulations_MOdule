# Original project context
Original name: Asta Fantacalcio Serie A 2026/27 — VOR.
Original preset: Serie A 2026/27, Classic, 12 participants, 500 credits, 3P–8D–8C–6A, defence modifier and goalkeeper clean-sheet bonus enabled.
Objective: maximize seasonal competitive squad value rather than win individual bids. Track calls, user/opponent purchases, sales, exclusions, budget and slots.

Previous method prioritized FantaLab projections/screenshots and official Fantacalcio.it roles/list values. Revised requirement: use a documented proprietary model for xFM, physical availability/integrità, selection, reliability and rated appearances; external forecasts are comparisons only. Users must select or confirm every league parameter. Weekly refresh plus injury and squad-selection event updates. Commercial goal: FantaBall subscription/auction assistant.

Legacy workbook: Fanta_VOR.xlsx. Visible columns included FMV Exp., Titolarità, Integrità, xAffidabilità, xPV, xPunti, replacement rank/window, VOR and xPrice. Visible values suggested xAffidabilità=0.6*Titolarità+0.4*Integrità, xPV=38*xAffidabilità/100 and xPunti=FMV Exp.*xPV; these were inferred relationships, not inspected formula cells. Do not treat workbook team mappings or predictions as current. Do not reuse that weighted score as an appearance probability without evaluation.

Original preset includes a defence modifier, but the demo does not implement it. The demo preset explicitly does not claim exact original-rule equivalence.
