# Objective and workflow
Exclude owned/excluded/ineligible players. Compare same-role substitutes, cheaper options enabling upgrades and feasible multi-player packages in shared worlds. Use realistic acquisition-cost distributions separately from fair value. Re-optimize remaining roster and budget under risk objective. Rank by net squad value, downside, fit and flexibility, not only points/price. Report cost, price band, squad value, probability better than target, savings, tradeoff and alternative price ceiling. Savings are contingent unless a feasible use is identified.
