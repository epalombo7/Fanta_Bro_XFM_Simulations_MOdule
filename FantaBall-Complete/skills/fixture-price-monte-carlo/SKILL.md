# Objective and workflow
Sample persistent player/team parameter uncertainty then chronological fixtures, physical availability/recovery, eligibility, selection/minutes and coherent scoring outcomes. Share worlds with alternatives; maintain injury blocks and team dependence. Derive rated appearances, points, fielded squad value, replacement and fair price per world with the pricing method. Report mean/median/P10/P25/P75/P90, probability value exceeds offer, seed/count/version/snapshot and convergence. No invented execution; no calibrated-confidence claim without validation. More draws do not fix poor assumptions.
