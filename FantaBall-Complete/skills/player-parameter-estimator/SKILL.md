# Objective and workflow
Verify evidence and retain provenance/timestamps. Estimate fit/recovery, eligibility, starting/substitute/valid-rating probability, minutes, base rating, attacking/assist/set-piece/discipline rates. Separate integrità, titolarità, affidabilità and confidence. Produce distributions and ranges. Never copy external xFM and call it proprietary; never invent a trained model. Avoid double penalizing injury through both appearances and a reliability multiplier.
