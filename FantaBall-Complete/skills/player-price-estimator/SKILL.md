# Objective and workflow
Read league, squad, ledger, available pool and forecast. Compare feasible playable squad strategies with/without player. Include formation, substitutes, risk, scarcity, modifiers and budget opportunity cost. Distinguish fair price, market acquisition estimate, target, rational maximum and legal maximum. Reserve minimum cost for all other mandatory slots. Quote/FVM is not primary value. Use executable deterministic calculations. Do not raise stop just because bids escalate.
